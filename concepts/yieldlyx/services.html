Process

How we work

From first call to production, a clear path with demos along the way.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We start with your goals, users, data sources, and constraints. A short discovery sprint surfaces the highest-value use cases and what success looks like.

  2. 02

    Plan & prototype

    We propose a phased roadmap, often a quick prototype or pilot, so you can validate before scaling budget. No six-month plans with nothing to show.

  3. 03

    Build

    Sprint-based delivery with demos every week. LLM features, ML pipelines, integrations, and UX, built to your security and compliance requirements.

  4. 04

    Deploy & integrate

    Production rollout with monitoring, auth, and handoff documentation. We wire into your environments and support your IT review process.

  5. 05

    Iterate & enable

    Post-launch tuning, eval improvements, and team enablement so your organization can own and extend what we built together.

Security

Security & responsible AI

AI only creates value when stakeholders trust it. We build with governance, access controls, and clear documentation from day one.

Data stays yours

We access only what the project requires, under least-privilege principles. Your proprietary data is not used to train models without explicit agreement.

Enterprise-ready practices

SSO, encrypted channels, audit logging, and compliance alignment where your industry requires it, built into delivery, not bolted on at the end.

Transparent architecture

We document how models are called, where data flows, and what gets logged, so your security and legal teams can review before go-live.
