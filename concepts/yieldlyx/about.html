Our company

About YieldlyX

YCSLINT is an AI-focused software studio helping enterprises and startups ship custom LLM products, ML systems, and enterprise integrations.

Mission

Our mission

YCSLINT exists to help organizations build AI that actually ships, not slide decks that stall.

YieldlyX partners with enterprises and startups from first discovery through production deployment, with a team that combines strategy, engineering, and product thinking.

We deliver custom AI and LLM product development, consulting and strategy, ML and data engineering, and enterprise integration, wired into how your team already works.

Values

What we believe

Principles that guide how we work with every client, startup or enterprise.

01

AI-native focus

We specialize in custom AI and LLM products, not general web dev with AI sprinkled on top. Strategy, engineering, and delivery in one team.

02

Small senior team

You work directly with strategists and engineers who ship. No account-manager layer between you and the people building your software.

03

Enterprise & startups

Fast MVPs for startups, governed rollouts for enterprises. We tailor process and scope to your stage, same approachable team either way.

04

Software studio, not a platform

We are a development partner, not a SaaS vendor holding your data. You own what we build together, with documentation and handoff built in.
