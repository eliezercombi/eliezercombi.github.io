FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does YieldlyX do?

YieldlyX is an AI-focused software studio. We help enterprises and startups design, build, and ship custom AI and LLM products, from early strategy through production deployment and integration with your existing tools.

What services do you offer?

Four core areas: custom AI and LLM product development, AI consulting and strategy, ML and data engineering, and enterprise AI integration (APIs, workflows, and internal tools). Most projects combine several of these.

Do you work with startups and enterprises?

Yes, both. Startups often need a focused MVP or pilot in weeks; enterprises need governance, security review, and phased rollouts. We tailor scope and process to your stage and constraints.

How is YieldlyX different from a generic dev shop?

We specialize in AI end-to-end, not just wrapping an API call. That means strategy, evaluation, data pipelines, UX for AI products, and integration with your stack. You get a small, senior team that ships, not a slide deck.

What kinds of AI products have you built?

We've delivered 48+ projects across 10+ industries, internal copilots, document intelligence, workflow automation, customer-facing assistants, and custom ML models. Every engagement starts with your data and constraints, not a template.

How do projects typically start?

Most begin with a discovery call: goals, users, data sources, and success metrics. We then propose a phased plan, often a short discovery sprint, a prototype, and production build, so you can validate before scaling spend.

Do you use our data securely?

Yes. We follow least-privilege access, encrypted channels, and your compliance requirements where applicable. We do not train on your proprietary data without explicit agreement. Details are covered in our privacy policy and project contracts.

Can you integrate with our existing systems?

That's a core part of what we do, REST and GraphQL APIs, SSO, CRMs, ticketing, data warehouses, and custom backends. AI only creates value when it fits how your team already works.

What is your tech stack?

We choose tools per project, common choices include Python, TypeScript, cloud ML platforms, vector databases, and leading LLM providers. We prioritize maintainability and what your team can operate after handoff.

How do we get started?

Email us at contact@yieldlyx.org or use the contact page. Share your goal, timeline, and any constraints. We'll reply with next steps, usually a short call to see if we're a good fit.
