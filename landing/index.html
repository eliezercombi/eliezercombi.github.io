How it works

Three steps, one of them is yours.

  1. 01

    Write the invoice

    Line items, due date, your tax details. Templates remember everything after the first one.

  2. 02

    Share one link

    Your client opens it, picks a network and pays from the wallet they already use. No sign-up on their side.

  3. 03

    Books close themselves

    Tally matches the transfer, marks the invoice paid and exports a clean ledger for your accountant.

FAQ

Questions clients ask us first.

Does my client need a Tally account?

No. They open the link, connect or scan with their wallet and pay. The whole thing takes about a minute.

What if they send the wrong amount?

Underpayments keep the invoice open with the balance shown. Overpayments are flagged so you can refund or credit them.

Can I still accept a bank transfer?

Yes. Add your bank details as a second option and mark the invoice paid manually when the money lands.

Is this a real product?

No. Tally is a concept made for this page, which is a design and front-end portfolio piece.
