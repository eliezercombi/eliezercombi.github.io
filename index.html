Scraper + alerts

Job Radar: only the posts worth reading

Watches public job feeds, keeps what matches your keywords and tells you once about each new post: in the terminal, as CSV or JSON, or straight into a Telegram chat. One broken source never stops the run.

  • Python standard library only
  • SQLite de-duplication
  • Whole-word matching
  • Retries with backoff

App for smart glasses + relay server

Aside: your task list in the corner of your eye

Today's tasks from a Notion database on Even G2 glasses: one per card, done or moved with a tap, new ones captured by voice and saved only after you confirm. A small relay server talks to Notion and to a language model, and falls back to plain rules when the model is unavailable.

  • TypeScript, 48 tests
  • Zero-dependency Node relay
  • End-to-end tests over real HTTP
  • Recorded in the device simulator

App for smart glasses + market data

Riskline: stop, target and P&L at a glance

A trading companion for the same glasses. Live prices, a gauge that shows where the price sits between your stop and your target, the actions that cut risk listed first, and a confirmation that spells out stop, target and risk before anything is sent. Runs on paper trading with live public market data.

  • TypeScript, 48 tests
  • Live public market data
  • Voice opens a confirmation, never an order
