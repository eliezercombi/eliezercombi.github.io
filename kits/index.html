FAQ

Before you ask.

What is the difference between the template and the kit?

The page is the same. The kit adds the things around it: colour presets, a pre-launch page, the share image and the checklist. If you are comfortable picking colours and writing a countdown yourself, the free template is enough.

Do I need to know how to code?

You edit text between HTML tags and replace one link. If that sounds like too much, the done-for-you option exists for that reason.

Does it work for a bot without a Mini App?

Yes. Point the button at t.me/your_bot and put a screenshot of the chat into the phone.

Why USDT?

It reaches me in minutes from anywhere, with no payment processor in between. Most people building on Telegram already have it.
